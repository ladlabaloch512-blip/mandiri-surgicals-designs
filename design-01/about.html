About Us

Mandiri Surgicals, Sialkot

Mandiri Surgicals is a manufacturer and exporter of surgical instruments based in Sialkot, Pakistan. Our certified scope covers General Surgery, Dental, Liposuction, Electro and Plastic Surgery instruments.

What We Make

We produce instruments for five ranges used in operating theatres, dental clinics and aesthetic practices. Production runs under certified quality management systems for both general quality and medical devices, with CE Class I conformity for the European market.

Buyers receive a written quotation for the exact items and quantities they enquire about. Certificate copies and product documents are available on request.
